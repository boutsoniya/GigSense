# GigSense: Organiser Interview Script

**Who to talk to (8-12 people):** college fest or cultural-club committee members, cafe/bar owners who host live music, small-venue managers, independent artists who self-organise gigs.

**Length:** 20-30 minutes. **Format:** call or in person. Take notes, and ask permission before recording.

**Golden rule:** Ask about what they *did*, not what they *would do*. Don't pitch your idea until the very end. People are polite and will say "sounds great" to anything.

---

## 1. Opening (2 min)

"Thanks for your time. I'm a student researching how people plan small music events. I'm not selling anything. I just want to understand how you actually do it, including what's messy. There are no right or wrong answers."

## 2. Background (3 min)

- Tell me about the last event you organised. What was it and when?
- What was your role? Who else was involved in decisions?
- How many events have you organised in total?

## 3. The decision (7 min)

- Walk me through how you decided to go ahead with that event.
- How did you decide the ticket price? Who had the final say?
- How did you estimate how many people would come?
- How did you decide how much to pay the artist or spend on the venue and sound?
- Was there any point where you almost cancelled or changed the plan? Why?

## 4. Money and outcomes (5 min)

- Roughly how much did the event cost, and how much did it bring in? (Ranges are fine.)
- How close was the actual turnout to what you expected?
- Has an event ever lost money or made much less than planned? What happened?
- What did you do differently afterwards?

## 5. Current tools (4 min)

- What do you use to plan the numbers: spreadsheet, notes, ticketing app, nothing?
- Show me, if you can, how that looks.
- What do you like about it? What annoys you?
- Have you ever tried another tool for this? Why did you stop?

## 6. Wishes and willingness (4 min)

- If you could know one thing before committing money to an event, what would it be?
- Have you ever paid for any tool or service to help with planning or ticketing? What and how much?
- Who would need to approve using a new tool (club head, college, owner)?

## 7. Light concept test (only now, 3 min)

"I'm exploring a simple tool where you enter ticket price, costs and sponsorship, and it shows your break-even turnout and the risk of a loss. Does that match something you'd actually want?"

- What would it need to do for you to use it for your next event?
- What would stop you from using it?
- Who else should I talk to?

## 8. Close

"Thank you. Can I follow up if I build a prototype? Would you be willing to share numbers from one past event (anonymised is fine)?"

## After 8-12 interviews, look for

- **Pain:** how many had lost money or been badly surprised by turnout?
- **Current workaround:** is it a spreadsheet, a guess, or nothing?
- **Behaviour over opinion:** did anyone actually pay for or build something to solve this?
- **Disconfirming evidence:** if most say "I just go with gut and it's fine," note that. It's a valuable finding, and it may mean pivoting the idea.
